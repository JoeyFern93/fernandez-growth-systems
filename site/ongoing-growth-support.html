Working together

A clear path from context to next steps.

You work directly with Joe, with the key decisions and responsibilities agreed together.

  1. Establish the starting point

    Review the setup and identify the responsibilities that need continued ownership. A scoped Review may help establish this.

  2. Agree on the working rhythm

    Define priorities, capacity, communication, review timing, fees, and the path for work outside the support scope.

  3. Improve and reassess

    Work through the agreed priorities, document changes, and revisit the plan as the business needs change.

A useful arrangement when

  • Your business needs recurring improvements and coordination.
  • You want one person to keep the wider system in view.
  • You have an internal decision-maker and can assign responsibility for customer follow-up.

We make the arrangement specific

  • Which systems and responsibilities Joe will look after.
  • The available capacity and agreed communication expectations.
  • The review rhythm and the information used to assess progress.
  • The fee, third-party costs, and how larger projects are scoped.
A few practical questions

Before we get started.

Does this mean replacing our existing vendors?
Existing vendors can remain involved. The support scope defines how Joe coordinates with them and which responsibilities stay with each person or team.
Is every future change included?
The agreement defines the responsibilities and capacity covered. Larger projects and additional requests are assessed and scoped before work begins.
What can we measure?
We use the information the setup can reliably provide. Reporting separates completed work and workflow checks from actual inquiries, qualified leads, and sales outcomes.
