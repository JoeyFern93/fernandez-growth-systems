Inquiry privacy

A clear explanation of what the form collects and why.

What you choose to share

The inquiry form asks for your name, work email, business name, the challenge you want to discuss, and a short message. Your website and service preference are optional. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, health information, or other sensitive information.

Why it is collected

Joe Fernandez uses the information to understand your situation, assess whether the services fit, respond to your inquiry, and discuss possible next steps. Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing or commit you to paid work.

Where inquiries are handled

The form uses Web3Forms to process the details you enter and deliver an inquiry notification to Joe's monitored inbox.

Web3Forms is configured to retain submissions in its dashboard for seven days. Copies delivered to Joe's email inbox are kept separately and are not automatically deleted by that setting.

Web3Forms and its service providers process technical information for delivery and spam prevention. Its policy says submitter IP and email addresses may be shared with spam-filtering providers. This form includes hCaptcha for a spam check; its script and service may process technical browser information when this page is opened. See Web3Forms privacy and hCaptcha privacy.

This site does not add advertising pixels or third-party analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request information to operate and protect its service.

Questions or corrections

Email joe@fernandezgrowthsystems.com to ask Joe about information you have submitted or request a correction or deletion. Include only the details needed to identify your inquiry.
