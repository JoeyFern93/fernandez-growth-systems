Inquiry privacy

A clear explanation of what the form collects and why.

What you choose to share

The inquiry form asks for your name, work email, business name, the challenge you want to discuss, and a short message. Your website and service preference are optional. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, health information, or other sensitive information.

Why it is collected

Joe Fernandez uses the information to understand your situation, assess whether the services fit, respond to your inquiry, and discuss possible next steps. Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing or commit you to paid work.

Where inquiries are handled

Inquiry collection is disabled in this preview. No inquiry details are sent from this copy. Before collection is enabled, this notice will identify the selected inquiry service and explain its processing and retention. This site does not add advertising pixels or third-party analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request information to operate and protect its service.

Questions or corrections

Use the inquiry form to ask Joe about information you have submitted or request a correction or deletion. Include only the details needed to identify your inquiry.
