Cast or mirror from this phone

To show the delayed replay on your TV, connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi and start mirroring before starting a new camera session.

  • Android with Google Cast — In Google Home, open Devices, press and hold your TV, then tap Cast and Cast my screen. If asked, share the browser where you will open Replay Cam.
  • iPhone with AirPlay — Open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and choose your TV.

Open Replay Cam on this phone to leave pairing. Tap Try demo to check the TV. Then tap Stop and Start camera.
