Build & Fix Sprint

A defined piece of work

  • Agreed priorities and deliverables
  • Clear responsibilities and dependencies
  • Checks against the agreed success criteria
  • Documentation and a practical handoff
The focus

Focused work where it matters most.

A Sprint combines the pieces needed for the agreed outcome. These are examples of work we can scope.

01

Website and inquiry paths

Clarify key pages, improve calls to action, and make it easier for the right visitors to take the next step.

02

Forms and lead routing

Improve the information collected and connect inquiries with the person or system responsible for responding.

03

CRM and follow-up

Organize stages, ownership, reminders, or automations around the way your team actually handles inquiries.

04

Source information and measurement

Connect agreed source fields and events so you can better trace inquiries through the process.

05

Tool and vendor coordination

Work through the dependencies between your platforms and vendors so the agreed changes connect.

06

Testing and documentation

Check the completed workflow, record how it works, and give the team a usable handoff.

What you receive

A change your business can operate.

The written scope makes the deliverables and responsibilities clear before work begins.

An agreed implementation scope

The work to be completed, the customer or operating problem it addresses, and the criteria we will use to assess completion.

The completed changes

The pages, integrations, workflows, or configurations included in the agreed scope, within the accounts you control.

A test and issues record

The checks performed, their results, and any remaining limitations or dependencies that affect the workflow.

A documented handoff

The setup, account ownership, operating steps, and next actions your team or vendors need to maintain the work.

Working together

A clear path from context to next steps.

You work directly with Joe, with the key decisions and responsibilities agreed together.

  1. Define the work

    Turn the priorities into a written scope with deliverables, timing, responsibilities, access, and investment.

  2. Build and connect

    Make the agreed changes, coordinate dependencies, and review the relevant decisions with you.

  3. Test and hand over

    Check the end-to-end workflow against the agreed criteria and walk your team through what changed.

A useful next step when

  • The main problem and desired outcome are clear enough to scope.

We define the project together

  • The exact pages, tools, integrations, or workflows being changed.
  • Completion criteria and the checks needed to verify them.
  • Your responsibilities, vendor dependencies, and approval points.
  • The project fee, timing, and treatment of additional requests.
A few practical questions

Before we get started.

Do we need a Review first?
Usually, a Review helps identify the right work. Existing findings or a clear project brief may provide enough context to scope implementation. Joe will assess that with you.
Will we have to replace our current platform?
Useful parts of your setup should stay. Any platform change needs a clear reason and an agreed scope that considers the effort and business impact.
What happens when the project is complete?
You receive the agreed handoff and can operate the setup internally or with your vendors. If continued coordination would help, we can discuss Ongoing Growth Support separately.
