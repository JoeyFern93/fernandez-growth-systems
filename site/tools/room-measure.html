Measurement summary

Measure two rooms

  1. Choose a room and units. Start with either blank room and name it under Room details.
  2. Draw its outline. Tap corners with Walls, then tap the first corner to close. Use Rectangle shortcut for a simple rectangular room.
  3. Record measured lengths. Choose Measure and tap each wall, or use the wall list. Fractions such as 6 1/2 inches are supported.
  4. Add doors, windows, bed, chair or notes. Move / edit lets you drag symbols or tap to edit. Freehand is for extra marks.
  5. Switch rooms and repeat. Then use Save / send to keep a project file, image, or editable HTML copy.

This is a manual recording tool, not a camera or laser measurement app. It does not infer distances from your finger movement.

Use inside wall-to-wall measurements. For an irregular room, record every wall and label alcoves. Only a rectangular room with entered length and width gets a calculated area.
