Cast or mirror this screen

The TV shows the delayed replay and controls from this device. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi and start mirroring before starting the camera.

  • Android with Google Cast — In Google Home, open Devices, press and hold your TV, then choose Cast and Cast my screen. If asked, share Replay Studio or the browser you use for it.
  • iPhone or iPad with AirPlay — Open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and select your TV.
  • Computer with Google Cast — In Chrome, open the three-dot menu, choose Cast, save, and share, then Cast. Select this tab as the source and choose your TV.

Return to Replay Studio and tap Try demo. When the moving timer appears on the TV, tap Stop, then Start camera. Choose Full screen for a larger view.

How to use Replay Studio
  1. Place your device where it can see the whole skill.
  2. Choose a delay that gives you time to return to the screen.
  3. Tap Start camera and allow camera access when your browser asks.
  4. Wait for the replay to appear, then practice and watch.
  5. Drag the Video timeline backward to pause and inspect a skill. Tap Save clip to trim that available footage, or Back to delay to practice again.

If the camera is blocked, check this site's camera permission in your browser. Open Replay Studio directly in Safari or Chrome.

Use the timeline with a finger, mouse, or arrow keys. Full screen keeps the review controls beside or below the video. Available delay, history, full screen, and clip recording depend on your device and browser. Compatibility mode uses a shorter history at lower quality. Switching apps or locking your screen stops the session and clears the rolling history; start again when you return.

PRIVACY

Where your video goes

Ordinary replay runs on this device. In a private TV room, your phone sends silent video to the paired display. If direct video stalls, the phone can automatically send encrypted video through the Fernandez Growth Systems relay. Select Direct only on the phone to opt out. Keep the phone link private because it contains the video key. Screen mirroring shares what you see with the TV you select.

Recent replay clears when you stop. Download clips and completed session recordings before closing or refreshing this page. Downloaded videos become normal files on this device, so follow your organization's recording, consent, and deletion policies.

How to install Replay Studio

Chrome on Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome and tap the three-dot menu.
  2. Tap Install and create shortcut, then Install. Some versions show Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Confirm the installation. Look for Replay Studio on your device.

If Chrome only offers Create shortcut, that option adds a home screen link that opens in Chrome.

Safari on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Share. In some layouts, tap the page menu first.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App if you see it, then tap Add.

On a computer

Chrome — Open the three-dot menu, choose Cast, save, and share, then Install page as app.

Edge — Open the three-dot menu, then choose More tools, Apps, and Install this site as an app.

Safari on Mac — Open the File menu and choose Add to Dock.

If your browser has no install option, you can bookmark Replay Studio and use it here.
